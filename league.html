UK League

The season-long competition for UK F5B pilots, across Beginners, Intermediate and Open F5B classes.

Format

The UK League runs a series of events across the season. All electric gliders are welcome, flying in one of three classes: Beginners, Intermediate, or Open F5B.

League placings are typically calculated from your best results across the season — for example, best 4 results from 6 events, or best 3 from 5, depending on how many events run that year.

This season's fixtures and standings are being finalised. Check back here for confirmed dates and venues, or email info@f5b.co.uk to be notified as soon as they're set.
